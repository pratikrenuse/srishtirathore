About Dr. Srishti Rathore

A specialist in conservative dentistry and endodontics, with a background in general practice, bioceramics research and undergraduate teaching.

Call +91 78883 551735.034 reviews on Google

Why she does this work

Dr. Rathore qualified as a dentist in Lucknow in 2017. During her BDS she was drawn to the treatments that save natural teeth, and that interest shaped everything after it.

She spent several years in general practice, then moved to Chandigarh for specialist training. She completed her MDS in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics at the Dr. Harvansh Singh Judge Institute of Dental Sciences and Hospital, Panjab University, in 2025. She has also trained in facial cosmetology.

In 2026 she opened her own clinic, Dant Chikitsak, in New Chandigarh. Her approach is simple: get to know the person, protect what can be protected, and offer the care that is needed at a fair price, with enough time for every patient.

Clinical focus

  • Root canal treatment, first time and re-treatment
  • Teeth with immature roots: apexification and apical barrier techniques
  • Vital pulp therapy and regenerative endodontic procedures
  • Managing complications such as separated instruments
  • Rotary and reciprocating NiTi instrument systems
  • Inlays, onlays and direct and indirect restorations
  • Aesthetic dentistry and rebuilding teeth after root canals

Teaching

  • Chairside clinical teaching of undergraduate students
  • Case discussions, seminars, journal clubs and clinical demonstrations
  • Mentoring interns and junior postgraduates
  • Pre-clinical and clinical examinations
