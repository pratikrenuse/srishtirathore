Complete dental care, with a specialist's focus on saving teeth

From a first filling to a difficult root canal, every treatment starts with the same question: what can be kept?

  • Root canal treatment

    Clears the infection inside a tooth and seals it, so the tooth stays in your mouth.

  • Root canal retreatment

    A second chance for a tooth where an earlier root canal has not healed or has become infected again.

  • Fillings, inlays and onlays

    Tooth coloured restorations that repair decay or cracks while removing as little tooth as possible.

  • Veneers and smile makeovers

    Repairs chipped, stained, gapped or uneven front teeth so they look natural.

  • Teeth whitening

    Professional whitening that lightens natural teeth under a dentist's supervision.

  • Scaling and polishing

    Professional cleaning that removes tartar and stain and keeps gums healthy.

  • Vital pulp therapy

    Keeps the nerve of a tooth alive when decay is deep, and can avoid a full root canal.

  • Treatment for young and injured teeth

    Apexification and regenerative procedures for permanent teeth whose roots have not finished forming.

What does an endodontist do?

An endodontist is a dentist with a three year postgraduate degree (MDS) in treating the inside of the tooth: the pulp and the root canals. Root canals, re-treatment of failed root canals and saving badly decayed teeth are the core of the work.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The tooth is numbed before treatment starts. Most patients say it feels like having a filling, and many of Dr. Rathore's Google reviews describe their root canal as pain-free.

Do you treat children?
