Veneers and smile makeovers in New Chandigarh

A smile makeover changes the colour, shape or spacing of the front teeth. It can be as small as repairing one chipped edge with tooth coloured composite, or as planned as a set of veneers. Dr. Rathore's speciality is conservative dentistry, so the plan starts with what can be kept.

What it can change

  • Front teeth that are discoloured and do not respond to whitening.
  • Gaps between teeth.
  • Chipped, worn or uneven edges.
  • Old fillings on front teeth that have stained or no longer match.
  • Teeth that look too small or out of proportion.

How it is done

  1. Consultation

    Photographs, an examination and a conversation about what you want to change.

  2. Plan

    You are shown the options: composite bonding, veneers, whitening, or a combination.

  3. Preparation

    Any decay or gum problem is treated first. For veneers, the teeth are prepared and an impression or scan is taken.

  4. Placement

    Composite is layered and shaped directly. Veneers are tried in, checked for shade and fit, then bonded.

  5. Review

    The bite and polish are checked and you are shown how to look after the result.

Aftercare

  • Avoid biting hard objects such as pens, nails or ice with restored front teeth.
  • Brush and floss as normal. Veneers and bonding still need clean gums around them.
  • Composite can pick up stain over the years. A polish at a check up refreshes it.

Common questions

What is the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding uses tooth coloured composite shaped directly on the tooth, usually in one visit. Veneers are thin shells made to fit and bonded to the front of the tooth. Bonding removes less tooth. Veneers hold their colour and polish longer.

Will my teeth be cut down?

It depends on the case. Some teeth need little or no preparation. Dr. Rathore will show you how much, if any, is needed before you decide.

Can I whiten my teeth instead?

If the only concern is colour, whitening is often tried first. Veneers or bonding are for shape, gaps, chips or stains that whitening cannot lift.
